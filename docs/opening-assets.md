# Opening artwork and asset provenance

Produced 27 October 2026 with the built-in image_gen tool (imagegen skill). Final artwork is in public/opening/scene-1.webp through scene-7.webp. Original generated PNGs were encoded as 1440-pixel WebP images for browser delivery. Captions, labels and controls are HTML; no readable text is embedded in the artwork.

## Hand

- Source: https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/blob/main/packages/assets/profiles/generic-hand/right.glb
- Upstream: immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles, packages/assets/profiles/generic-hand/right.glb
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2019 Amazon. Full license retained at public/opening/HAND-LICENSE.md and distributed with the build.
- Original skinned mesh and 25-joint skeleton retained. Finger chains are attached at their original bind pose; joint rotations and a warm porcelain material create the opening gestures.
- No paid asset or external video service was used. No Zero artwork, hand model, shader, branding, or code was copied.
